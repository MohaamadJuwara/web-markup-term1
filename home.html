Your first trip to the Gambia

Most first-timers land in Banjul and head straight for the coast, but the best introduction to the country happens a little further out.

The Gambia is the smallest country on mainland Africa, a thin strip of land along the Gambia River. It is called the Smiling Coast because of how warmly visitors are welcomed.

This guide walks you through what you actually need to know before landing: how to greet people, what to eat, where to go and what to listen to once you step outside the resort.

Good to know

  • The currency is the dalasi. Hotels often quote prices in € or £, but markets only take dalasi.
  • English is the official language. Wolof and Mandinka are spoken everywhere.
  • The dry season, from November to May, is the best time to visit.
