About this site

Smiling Coast is a free travel guide to The Gambia for first-time visitors. I made it as a student project. The site has no ads and sells nothing.

The tips on this site

The tips about culture, food, places and music come from my own experience. They are meant to help you prepare, not as official advice. Prices, opening times and rules can change, so always check official sources before you travel, especially for visas, health and safety.

Photos, videos and recordings

The videos were made by me and my family at gatherings and ceremonies in The Gambia. The performance by Demba Tanja is shared with respect for his work. Please do not copy or reuse any photos, videos or recordings from this site without asking first.
