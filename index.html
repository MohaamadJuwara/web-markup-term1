Shopping at the side market

The side market sells fresh produce, fabric and handwoven baskets. Most stalls are busiest in the morning and start closing by early afternoon. Prices aren't fixed, so offer roughly half of what's asked and expect to land somewhere in the middle. Keep small notes on hand, since vendors often can't break large bills.
