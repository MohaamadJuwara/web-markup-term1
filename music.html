Music & Stories

In The Gambia, history is not only written down, it is played and sung. Drums welcome guests, the kora fills the evening, and griots keep family stories alive.

Watch & listen

What is a griot?

Griots are families of storytellers and musicians. For generations, they have kept the history of families and communities alive by telling it, singing it and playing it, and passing it on from parent to child.
