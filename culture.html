Culture & Manners

A little cultural knowledge goes a long way. Here's how to greet people, what to wear and how bargaining works, so you feel confident, not awkward, on day one.

  • Greetings

    Expect a proper handshake using your right hand. Greetings here aren't rushed.

  • Ask after family

    Don't be surprised if someone asks about your family or your health before getting to the point. It's a sign of respect, not small talk to skip through.

  • Dress for town

    Cover your shoulders and knees. Light, breathable fabrics work fine in the heat.

  • In the village

    Away from the beach, life looks like this, sandy roads, family compounds and neighbours greeting each other. Swap your swimwear for a T-shirt and shorts or a light dress before you head inland.

  • Bargaining at roadside or markets

    Haggling is expected and genuinely friendly. Start below what you're willing to pay and meet somewhere in the middle.

  • Fixed price shops

    Fixed price shops and supermarkets are the exception, no bargaining needed there.
